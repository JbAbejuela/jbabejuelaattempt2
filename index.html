About me

I’m Jb S. Abejuela, a computing student learning how interfaces are put together, from colour and type choices to the HTML underneath. I like taking a polished product page apart to see why it works.

For this project I studied the design systems behind Slack and Firefox, then rebuilt their colour palettes, type scales and components in live HTML and CSS.
